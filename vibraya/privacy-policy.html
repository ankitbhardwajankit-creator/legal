Vibraya

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Vibraya is made by Mehank Visionary Solutions Pvt Ltd. It has no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that stores your data. Your journal, intentions and streaks stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what our payment provider needs to check whether you have Premium. This policy explains exactly what that is, where it goes and why.

What we do not collect

Vibraya has no user accounts, no login, no email or phone collection, no advertising SDKs, and no analytics or tracking libraries. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers; there is no advertising in the app.

Permissions this app requests, and why

On Android, Vibraya requests: Foreground Service and Foreground Service (Media Playback), which show a media notification with playback controls so your session keeps playing when the screen is off or you switch apps; Wake Lock, which keeps that audio playing while your phone sleeps; Modify Audio Settings, which every audio app needs to manage volume and audio focus (it gives no access to anything you say or record); Internet and network state, used to check your Premium status and load prices from Google Play and RevenueCat; and Google Play Billing, used to make purchases. If you layer your own music under a frequency (a Premium feature), your phone's own file picker opens and we receive only the single file you pick, which is played on your phone and never uploaded. Vibraya never requests camera, microphone, contacts, location, calendar or SMS access.

What stays on your device

Your daily intentions, journal entries, streaks and app settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We never receive them. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, they are permanently deleted. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in your own Google account backup; we cannot access that backup.

What leaves your device: Premium status and purchases

Each time the app starts, and when you open the Premium screen, buy or restore, the app contacts RevenueCat, Inc. (our purchase-management provider) and Google Play to check whether you have Premium and to load current prices. This sends: a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app; your purchase history for this app (products bought, dates, renewal status and Google Play order details); and basic device and app information (such as platform, operating-system version, app version, language and store country). It does not include your name, email, phone number or payment card details; Google Play handles payment and keeps those. We use this data only to give you the Premium features you paid for, let you restore them on a new phone, and keep the records required by law. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Where this data is processed

RevenueCat, Inc. processes this data on our behalf, on servers in the United States. Google processes payments under its own privacy policy. By making a purchase, you consent to this transfer outside India for the purpose described above. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept while you have Premium and afterwards for as long as needed to restore your purchase, handle refunds and disputes, and meet tax and accounting law. If you ask us to erase your purchase data, we ask RevenueCat to delete it, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Children

Vibraya is meant for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process children's data. If you believe a child has made a purchase, contact us and we will erase the related purchase data.

Data security

Your journal and intentions never leave your phone, so there is no server-side copy of them to breach. Communication between the app, Google Play and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not store payment card details anywhere.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us for a summary of the personal data we process about you, ask us to correct or erase it, withdraw your consent, nominate someone to exercise these rights for you, and raise a grievance with us. Data on your phone you can erase yourself at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. For the purchase data described above, email mehankvisionarysolutions@gmail.com with the Google Play order number of your purchase. Withdrawing consent or erasing purchase data means we can no longer confirm your Premium access on a new phone. If you are not satisfied with our response to a grievance, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Grievance contact

Grievance Officer, Mehank Visionary Solutions Pvt Ltd. Email: mehankvisionarysolutions@gmail.com. We acknowledge grievances within 7 days and aim to resolve them within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "last updated" date below shows the latest version. Material changes also appear in the app's Legal screen with the new date.
